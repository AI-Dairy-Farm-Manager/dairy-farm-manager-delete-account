🐄 Account Deletion Request – AI Dairy Manager

If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, please follow the steps below.

📧 Send a deletion request by email

We will manually delete your authentication record from our system within 7 days.

Send an email to: AI-Dairy-Farm-Manager@outlook.com with the following information:

  • Subject line: Account Deletion Request – AI Dairy Manager
  • Body: Your registered email address (the one you used to sign in)
  • (Optional) A brief reason for deletion – helpful for us to improve.

📌 What happens after you request deletion?

  1. We will verify that the email address belongs to an active account.
  2. Your authentication data (email, password hash, phone number if provided) will be permanently removed from Firebase.
  3. We will send you a confirmation email once the deletion is completed.
  4. Local farm data (cattle, milk records, etc.) stored on your device will not be deleted by us – you can uninstall the app to remove it from your device.

⚠️ Note: After your account is deleted, you will no longer be able to sign in. If you reinstall the app, you will need to create a new account. Any data you had saved locally on your device will still be accessible until you clear the app data or uninstall the application.

🔄 Alternative: Delete account directly from the app (future update)

We are working on adding an in‑app "Delete Account" button in a future release, which will allow you to delete your account instantly without email. Until then, please use the email method above.
